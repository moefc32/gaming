Nyanpash

ニャンパッシュ

noun — personal term

A fondness for cats that borders on infatuation. The name comes from nyan, the sound of a cat's meow, and pash, an infatuation or strong admiration. Somewhere along the way, it became a corner of the internet that grew into an online presence across different interests and everything in between.

Also, there might just be another reason the name feels familiar. Perhaps you love Non Non Biyori as well?
